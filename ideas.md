# Optimum Physio — Website Design Directions

## Three possible directions

### 1. Clinical Prism

**Very Brief Intro:** A deep indigo presentation canvas where frosted glass panels reveal the brand system as a calm, editorial sequence. It feels like a premium clinical playbook rather than a conventional agency deck.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Motion Atlas

**Very Brief Intro:** A bright, white-gallery website organised around range-of-motion arcs, pin-like markers, and measured visual diagrams. It expresses physiotherapy through clarity, movement, and useful instruction.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Recovery Ledger

**Very Brief Intro:** An understated document-inspired system that combines large type, white space, and annotated image evidence. It feels considered and operational, like a beautifully edited client dossier.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Clinical Prism

### Design Movement

**Clinical Prism** draws from contemporary Swiss editorial systems and restrained glassmorphism. It avoids the “futuristic app” version of glass: blur, translucency, and light are used only to create hierarchy and depth around a real clinical story.

### Core Principles

1. **Glass has a job.** A frosted panel separates a message from imagery or groups a functional decision; it never becomes decorative clutter.
2. **White space signals trust.** Each section has one dominant idea and generous breathing room, so the client can understand the system quickly.
3. **Purple owns the stage; cyan shows motion.** The supplied circular logo anchors the identity, while cyan acts as a controlled movement cue.
4. **Evidence becomes presentation.** The site behaves like a client-ready visual system, mixing polished rules with concrete component demonstrations.

### Color Philosophy

The base is a deep indigo-to-purple field that gives the brand confidence and allows the light glass components to feel deliberate. Frost white and lilac mist create a calm clinical reading surface. Cyan is kept rare and kinetic, signalling movement, progression, and interactive moments. Coral remains an occasional alert accent rather than a decorative colour.

### Layout Paradigm

The site uses a **presentation spine** rather than a centred marketing-page grid. A slim, translucent vertical rail carries the section marker and page rhythm on desktop, while large asymmetric sections alternate from full-bleed dark canvas to airy frosted editorial pages. Content cards float into the available space rather than repeating a uniform card grid.

### Signature Elements

1. A cyan range-of-motion arc that travels through hero and section transitions.
2. Frosted glass specification cards with low-opacity borders and soft internal light.
3. Numbered “system plates” that make each rule or template feel like a considered design artifact.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should make the system easier to explore: nav links glide to sections, palette swatches copy a colour value, and component demonstrations reveal a concise implementation note. Hover states subtly lift and brighten glass panels without changing the calm hierarchy.

### Animation

Use subtle, low-frequency motion: the hero arc drifts slightly, translucent orbs move slowly below the composition, and section content enters with a 30–60 ms stagger. Components respond to hover using transform and opacity only. All motion must respect reduced-motion preferences and stay under 300 ms for direct interaction.

### Typography System

Use **Manrope** for confident sans-serif headlines and interface copy, **DM Sans** for readable paragraphs, and **IBM Plex Mono** for compact system labels, page markers, HEX values, and implementation notes. Headlines use sentence case, compact line lengths, and strong size contrast; body copy remains quiet and generous.

### Brand Essence

**A client-ready social design system that turns Optimum Physio’s movement expertise into a calm, recognisable digital presence.**

**Personality:** precise, reassuring, contemporary.

### Brand Voice

Headlines should be direct and assured. CTAs should feel like useful guidance, not marketing pressure. Microcopy should sound operational and considered.

Example lines: “A calmer system for content that needs to earn trust.”

Example lines: “One message. One motion cue. One clear next step.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The supplied circular Optimum mark remains the visual anchor. It should be displayed at a confident, readable size in the header and hero, surrounded by clear space. The website should not invent a replacement wordmark; “OPTIMUM / VISUAL SYSTEM” functions as an editorial descriptor rather than a new logo.

### Signature Brand Color

**Optimum Purple — `#4B4895`** is the ownable colour that anchors every major system state.

## Client Brief Addendum

The website now has two distinct destinations. The existing **Brand System** remains intact as the visual reference. The default destination becomes an **In-Depth Client Brief**, which applies the same Clinical Prism direction to a more operational reading experience: the September content programme, individual scripts and CTAs, production workflow, approval inputs, web-domain condition, deferred security-testing status, and recovery decisions.

The Client Brief should feel like an executive project dossier rather than a marketing landing page. It will keep the indigo-to-frost section cadence, mono system labels, cyan motion cues, and restrained glass cards. However, its primary structural device is an expandable **brief ledger**: individual post cards expose the full creative brief, CTA, production note, and status without forcing the client to leave the page. The domain-status portion is visually distinct and precise: it presents the verified unavailable state, the required recovery path, the fact that authorised security testing is deferred until a reachable domain exists, and the user-provided qualification that this next phase may require additional charges.
