# Strategy Site — Initial Audit Findings

## Source

The supplied Optimum Physio strategy hub presents a September 2026 social content programme for **Optimum Physio Rehab & Wellness**. Its stated purpose is to make the clinic more discoverable, trusted, and easier to choose through clinical guidance, transparent care journeys, and informed next steps.

## Visible programme structure

The strategy identifies ten posts scheduled from 1–30 September 2026, at a cadence of two to three posts per week. It assigns the following content mix: **40% Educational, 30% Patient Proof, 20% Modality, and 10% Clinician Branding**. It also provides a five-stage campaign flow, caption/CTA guidance, an approval checklist, production batches, and a measurement framework.

## Cross-calendar production rules

Every caption follows **hook → useful context → appropriate boundary → one clear next step**. CTA types are save/share for education; comment or DM keyword for patient stories and audience research; and booking for first-visit or treatment spotlight posts. The supplied DM boundary states that social messages cannot assess or diagnose, and routes the person to booking with a physiotherapist.

The production approach groups posts 1, 2, 3, and 6 as an education shoot batch; posts 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, and 10 as trust-and-process content that requires consent; and supplementary stories as short, low-edit publishing-day assets. Mandatory client inputs include exact services, clinician credentials and scripts, written patient consent, local booking/contact details, accessibility assets, and clinical review.
