# Optimum Physio — Client Brief Source Record

## Source scope

This record consolidates the supplied September 2026 strategy hub and the attached **Website Condition and Build-History Audit**. It is the factual content basis for the client-brief website rebuild.

## Content calendar — September 2026

The strategy describes a ten-post programme for Optimum Physio Rehab & Wellness, scheduled from 1–30 September 2026 at a cadence of two to three posts per week. It uses a **40 / 30 / 20 / 10** content system: 40% Educational, 30% Patient Proof, 20% Modality, and 10% Clinician Branding. Its stated approach is: useful clinical guidance earns attention; transparent care journeys turn attention into confident enquiries.

## Caption, CTA, and production framework

Every caption follows **hook → useful context → appropriate boundary → one clear next step**.

| CTA type | Intended use | Example from source |
|---|---|---|
| Save or share | Educational reels and carousels | “Save this for your next desk day.” |
| Comment or DM keyword | Patient stories and audience research | “DM ‘MOVE’ to ask how to book an assessment.” |
| Book now | First-visit and treatment-spotlight content | “Book an assessment at `[BOOKING LINK]`.” |

**DM boundary template:** “Thank you for getting in touch. We cannot assess or diagnose through social messages, but we can help you arrange an appointment to discuss your goals with a physiotherapist. You can book here: `[BOOKING LINK]`.”

The source divides content collection into three production batches. **Batch A — Education** covers posts 1, 2, 3, and 6 in a half-day using desk, stair, chair, and gym setups with vertical video and stills for covers. **Batch B — Trust & process** covers posts 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, and 10 after consent and includes reception, assessment, conversation, and treatment details. **Batch C — Stories** is publishing-day support: low-edit clips for polls, Q&As, clinic walkthroughs, and World PT Day reminders.

The source approval inputs are: exact service menu and modality availability; clinician names, credentials, and final scripts; written patient consent and final wording approval; `[CITY]`, `[BOOKING LINK]`, phone number, hours, and availability; captions, alt text, subtitles, and visual accessibility; and clinical review of exercise and treatment descriptions.

## Website condition, domain, and security-test status

The attached audit, reviewed on 16 August 2026, concludes that **optimumphysiorehab.com was not publicly reachable at the time of review**. The apex and `www` hostnames did not resolve through the tested public DNS path, HTTPS could not be established, and the observed public-DNS response was an NXDOMAIN condition. Because a public server endpoint was not reachable, the audit could not inspect page content, forms, mobile behaviour, performance, accessibility, security headers, analytics, CMS/plugins, or TLS configuration.

The audit’s key operational conclusion is that the domain must be clarified or restored before the intended website can be launched and before an authorised external security test can meaningfully proceed. The client-brief wording requested for the website is: **“We will reconfigure the domain, or may need to purchase it again if it is no longer recoverable. This work, together with the deferred authorised security-testing stage, will require additional charges.”** No cost amount is assumed in this brief.

The audit recommends that the owner first check registrar status, renewal date, nameservers, and DNS zone. If the domain remains registered, it should be pointed to the selected hosting service with valid DNS records; if it is unregistered, availability and commercial/legal suitability should be confirmed before purchase. The required pre-launch checks include consistent apex/www resolution or a clean canonical redirect, valid HTTPS, working mobile contact actions and booking routes, a responsive page, accessible forms and navigation, meta data/sitemap/robots/local-business schema, analytics with privacy notices, and documented backups.
